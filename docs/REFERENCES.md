# References

The [1995 DPhil thesis](SL_DPhil_Thesis_1995.pdf) is included with the author's
permission. Other papers are linked to their publishers or preprint records
rather than redistributed here. Publisher access may require a subscription;
preprints can differ from the published versions.

## SK equilibrium, replicas and replica symmetry breaking

  [Publisher / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/0305-4470/11/5/028).
- G. Parisi (1980), **A sequence of approximated solutions to the S-K model
  for spin glasses**. Journal of Physics A 13, L115.
  [Publisher / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/0305-4470/13/4/009).
- G. Parisi (1980), **The order parameter for spin glasses: a function on
  the interval 0-1**. Journal of Physics A 13, 1101.
  [Publisher / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/0305-4470/13/3/042).
- G. Parisi (1983), **Order Parameter for Spin-Glasses**.
  Physical Review Letters 50, 1946.
  [Publisher / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.50.1946).
- M. Talagrand (2006), **The Parisi formula**. Annals of Mathematics 163,
  221-263. [Publisher / DOI](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2006.163.221).
- P. Charbonneau (2022), **From the replica trick to the replica symmetry

- G. Parisi (2023), **Nobel Lecture: Multiple equilibria**.
  [arXiv:2304.00580](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.00580).
- D. Sherrington and S. Kirkpatrick (2025), **50 years of spin glass theory**.
  Nature Reviews Physics 7, 528-529.
  [Publisher / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42254-025-00871-z);

  [arXiv:2510.01054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01054).
